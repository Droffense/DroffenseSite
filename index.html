Mission-ready systems

Four families of modular payload hardware — every device controlled directly from the DJI remote, built to integrate, swap, and deploy in the field.

Airdrop Systems

Dropper Gen. 4

High-load, easy-to-use dropping device with multiple safety features and seamless DJI Pilot integration.

  • Up to 40 kg carrying capacity
  • Single / double package modes
  • OTA firmware-upgradeable

Stabilizer Systems

Stabilizer Mega / Mini

Cargo stabilizers for high-altitude hoist ops — automatic heading-keeping with visual lock.

  • Loads up to 20 kg (Mega) / 5 kg (Mini)

  • Works with all end-effectors
